Snowy Better Leaves - compatibility patch

Puts Winter Pack's snowy leaf textures on Better Leaves' bushy leaf models.

Pack order (top = highest priority):
  1. Snowy Better Leaves (this)
  2. Winter Pack
  3. Better Leaves

Snow that only sits on the top layer of a tree needs Continuity (or OptiFine), same as Winter Pack.
Leaf types: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark_oak, mangrove, cherry. Other leaves (azalea, pale oak, ...) stay as plain Better Leaves.

Credits: Winter Pack by Futureazoo; Better Leaves by Motschen (MIT). Personal-use patch.
